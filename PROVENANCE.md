# Provenance

The initial `0.1.0` package was extracted from the Signapse repository workflow on 2026-09-18 and generalized for reuse. It includes locally maintained adaptations of the following skills and their bundled resources:

- `explore`, `grilling`, `grill-with-docs`, `to-spec`, `to-ticket`, `implement`, `tdd`, `code-review`, and `domain-modeling`
- `diagnosing-bugs`, `verify-and-log-bug`, `resolving-merge-conflicts`, `research`, `prototype`, and `codebase-design`
