# Business analysis guidance

Apply business analysis in proportion to uncertainty inside the current flow. BACCM is a thinking frame, not six required headings.

- When the solution direction could change, establish the accepted need, affected stakeholders, outcome assumptions, meaningful exceptions, and trade-offs. Do not convert hypotheses into accepted requirements.
- When drafting or publishing a contract, distinguish verification of requirement quality from validation against the accepted need and value. Surface material gaps without reopening settled decisions.
- Preserve traceability from need to Epic outcome, Story acceptance criteria, Task coverage, and verification evidence when those levels exist. Do not create artificial parents or a separate matrix by default.
- For outcomes that need later evaluation, identify the observation or evidence source, evaluator, and timing only when the accepted need requires them. Do not invent KPIs or telemetry.
- Add migration, continuity, or operational guidance only when required to reach the accepted future state.
- A prototype reports the question tested, result, evidence limits, and open decisions. Its result does not authorize production implementation or publication.
- A Bug deviation may be established by reproduction or sufficient incident evidence in the requested environment and actor context. Preserve Expected Behavior and its Basis; do not invent reproduction steps.
